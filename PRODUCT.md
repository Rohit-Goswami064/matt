# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Skinny-fat men who feel stuck between “not overweight” and “not fit.” They arrive from YouTube or Instagram, often after years of programs that did not stick, and they are deciding whether to book a 1-on-1 call with Matt.

## Product Purpose

A coaching landing page that sells Matt Nguyen’s 3–6 month transformation system. Success is a booked call — earned by making the before/after body change feel physical, personal, and inevitable.

## Brand Personality

Physical, direct, earned. Polaroid proof over polish. Confidence without gym-bro swagger.

## Anti-references

Generic fitness funnels with neon CTAs and stock abs. SaaS-cream coaching templates. Bounce-heavy “wow” motion that draws attention to itself. Fade-up-on-scroll on every section.

## Design Principles

- Show the body change; do not describe it.
- Before and after should read as two sides of one story, not a gallery.
- Motion should feel like photographs being placed on a table, then picked up.
- One signature hero moment beats scattered micro-interactions.
- Copy stays plain and first-person; the photos carry the drama.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` with an instant visible state instead of choreography. Keep text contrast on the dark ink ground. Do not trap keyboard focus during hero motion. Hover-grow is pointer-only; keyboard focus still lifts the active tile.
